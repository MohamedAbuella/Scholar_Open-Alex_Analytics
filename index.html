Query tips: words typed plainly are matched against title and abstract and ranked by relevance. Use capital-letter operators to refine: AND requires both terms (solar AND forecasting), OR accepts either (irradiance OR "PV power"), NOT excludes a term (forecasting NOT wind). Put "quotes" around an exact phrase, use parentheses to group ((solar OR photovoltaic) AND forecast*), and * as a wildcard (forecast* covers forecast, forecasting, forecasts). Lowercase "and"/"or" are treated as ordinary words. Advanced options narrow the search to an author, affiliation, publication (journal), country (2-letter code such as GB or US), work type or minimum citations; separate several authors, affiliations or publications with ;. Names are matched to the best OpenAlex entry, shown in the log below, and the query box may be left empty when using them.

Method (mirrors the review study)

Q1: journals, authors and influential publications from the top OpenAlex results (counting, citation outliers per year by the 1.5×IQR rule). Q2: frequent abbreviations (neighbourhood-search matching) and words (term frequency, TF-IDF, n-grams, correlations). Q3: topics via LDA. Papers are then ranked by an alignment score and the best are saved as a .bib database.

Data come from the free OpenAlex API: the most relevant works for your query with abstracts, citation counts and venues. Results are saved as OpenAlex_Keyword_N_Mon_Year.bib, using the query keyword most frequent in the results, the number of papers kept and the month and year (e.g. OpenAlex_Hydrogen_60_Nov_2026.bib).
