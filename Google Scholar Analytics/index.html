Search Google Scholar & collect results

Advanced options (author, publication, phrase, exclusions…)

Query tips: type the words to search for; Scholar requires all of them. Use quotes for an exact phrase ("cloud motion"), OR in capitals for alternatives (irradiance OR insolation) and a minus sign to exclude a word (forecasting -wind). Advanced options narrow the search to an author, a publication (journal), an exact phrase, alternative or excluded words, title-only matches, review articles, or a minimum number of citations. Scholar has no affiliation or country filter. Results are saved as Scholar_Keyword_N_Mon_Year.bib, using the query keyword most frequent in the results, the number of papers kept, and the month and year (e.g. Scholar_Hydrogen_60_Nov_2026.bib).

Method (mirrors the review study)

Q1: journals, authors and influential publications from the top Scholar results (counting, citation outliers per year by the 1.5×IQR rule). Q2: frequent abbreviations (neighbourhood-search matching) and words (term frequency, TF-IDF, n-grams, correlations). Q3: topics via LDA. Papers are then ranked by an alignment score and the best are saved as a .bib database.

Scholar has no API and may show a CAPTCHA. If so, open Scholar once in a normal tab, solve it, and press Run again; collected results are kept and the search resumes.
